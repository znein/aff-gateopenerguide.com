Gate opener size guide

Every gate opener is rated for a maximum gate length and weight. Get those two numbers right and the choice is easy.

Size your gate in three steps

  1. Measure the length of the gate itself, end to end – not the opening.
  2. Find the weight. Your gate's installer or fabricator can usually tell you; for a double swing gate, size each leaf.
  3. Choose an opener rated above both – and leave some headroom, since a gate that's near the limit works the motor hard.

Then think about traffic. A home gate opens a few times a day; an apartment complex or business entrance may open hundreds of times. The commercial and APT models are built for that.
