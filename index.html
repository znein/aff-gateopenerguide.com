Questions people ask

Slide or swing – which opener do I need?

It depends on your gate. A sliding gate rolls sideways along a track and needs a slide gate operator; a swinging gate hinges open like a door and needs a swing gate operator. Double swing gates need a dual model such as the Eagle 200 DM.

How do I pick the right size?

Measure your gate's length and estimate its weight, then choose an opener rated above both. For example, the Eagle-1 handles slide gates up to 18 ft and 300 lb, the Eagle-1000 up to 27 ft and 600 lb, and the Eagle-2000 series up to 45–50 ft and 2,000–2,500 lb.

What happens in a power outage?

Many of these openers have a manual release: the Eagle II and Eagle-100 swing openers have quick-release mechanisms, and the crank-to-open slide models let you crank the gate open by hand.

Do I need a safety sensor?

A photo eye like the EG360 stops the gate from closing on a vehicle that's halfway through, and it's UL 325 compliant. The Eagle-1 and Eagle-2000-FR above already include one.
